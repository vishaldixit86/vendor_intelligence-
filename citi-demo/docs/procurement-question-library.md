## Vendor Review

* `Should we renew Aurelix Codeworks?`
* `What applications does Aurelix Codeworks support?`
* `What is the budget, actual, and forecast for Aurelix Codeworks?`
* `What should we negotiate with Aurelix Codeworks?`
* `Who are the competitors of Aurelix Codeworks?`

## Comparison

* `Compare Aurelix Codeworks and Velmora Cloud Operations.`
* `Compare Aurelix Codeworks and Velmora Cloud Operations on cost and SLA performance.`
* `Which vendor is performing best?`

## Evidence Follow-Ups

After a vendor or comparison answer, ask:

* `Explain all evidence values and calculations.`
* `How is YTD variance derived?`
* `How did you derive the SLA status?`
* `What is the annual base fee?`
* `What is the service category?`
* `What is the reporting period?`
* `Which one is stronger overall?`

## Interpretation Boundaries

The workbench explains source values, deterministic calculations, and evidence limitations. It does not infer unavailable incident trends, delivery acceptance, legal rights, market benchmarks, replacement capacity, or supplier willingness.
